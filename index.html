FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an invisible safety grill?

It is a set of thin, high-tensile stainless steel cables fixed in aluminium tracks. It protects balconies and windows but does not block the view.

What is the price of invisible grill in Patna?

Price depends on area (per sq ft), cable gap and material grade. Call or WhatsApp 7061899614 for a free site visit and exact estimate.

Is it strong enough for children and pets?

Yes. Each cable is tensioned tightly and the gap is kept small (usually 2–3 inch), so kids and pets cannot pass through.

Will it rust in rain?

No. We use SS 316 marine-grade steel with nylon coating, which is made for outdoor and humid conditions.

How long does installation take?

Most balconies and windows are finished within one day by our team.

Do you work outside Patna?

Yes, HR Home Safety serves all 38 districts of Bihar, including Gaya, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur, Darbhanga, Begusarai and Purnia.
